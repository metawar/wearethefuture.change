Future Is Ours

AI is replacing people faster than society is preparing for. If we don’t act now, job loss becomes normal, and millions will join the precariat.

Contact your MP today Watch the video Join the coalition

Pro-people. Pro-stability. Pro-future.

Why this matters

  • Automation isn’t “efficiency” if it destroys livelihoods.
  • “Cheaper goods” means nothing if people can’t earn.
  • Policy is lagging years behind deployment.
  • This hits the middle and working class first, and hardest.
  • Without safeguards, we trade human dignity for shareholder margins.

What we’re calling for

  • Transparency: disclose AI job replacement + publish impact numbers.
  • Fair transition: training linked to real jobs + wage protection.
  • Human-first rules: limits in critical services + accountability for harm.

The action kit: do this today

Step 1 — Find your MP: members.parliament.uk/FindYourMP

Step 2 — Email your MP (copy/paste):

Subject: Urgent: AI job displacement needs UK protections now

Step 3 — Call script (30 seconds):

“Hi, I’m [NAME], a constituent in [POSTCODE]. I’m calling about AI and automation replacing jobs with no protections. I’d like the MP to support transparency, fair transition support, and human-first safeguards. Can you log this and tell me how the MP will respond?”

Step 4 — Request a meeting:

Ask for a short meeting/phone call to discuss AI job displacement and local impacts.

Step 5 — Share publicly (optional):

“AI is replacing jobs faster than policy is responding. I’ve contacted my MP. If you’re in the UK, do the same: members.parliament.uk/FindYourMP #FutureIsOurs #HumanFirst”
